# eunwon Design Partner Recruitment Playbook

Last updated: 2026-09-05  
Owner: Founder / Growth  
Goal: Recruit 30 founder candidates and 20 advisor/accelerator candidates, complete discovery interviews, and validate a paid Application Sprint without claiming unproven outcomes.

## 1. Operating rules

- This is a recruitment and learning program, not a mass-email campaign.
- Research every candidate before contact. Record a public, recent trigger and its source URL.
- Prefer warm introductions, then verified work email, then LinkedIn; do not scrape or guess private contact details.
- Do not claim selection-rate improvements, customer counts, time savings, or customer logos until documented and permissioned.
- Do not imply that eunwon guarantees eligibility, application acceptance, or funding.
- Use `eunwon` as the product name and describe the service as a government-support application workflow, not merely an AI search tool.
- Stop outreach immediately after an opt-out. Retain only the minimum record needed to honor that opt-out.
- The founder reviews and approves the first 20 outbound messages and every paid-pilot commitment.

### 2.3 Candidate scoring

Score each candidate before outreach. Contact highest scores first; require at least 65/100.

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring guide |
|---|---:|---|
| Active need | 30 | 30: deadline/application within 60 days; 20: public funding/search activity; 10: plausible but unconfirmed; 0: none |
| ICP fit | 25 | 25: matches all criteria; 15: one material mismatch; 5: exploratory |
| Workflow frequency | 15 | 15: several applications/clients yearly; 8: occasional; 0: one-off/unknown |
| Reach/distribution | 10 | 10: portfolio/community reach; 5: can refer peers; 0: individual only |
| Researchable trigger | 10 | 10: specific event in last 90 days; 5: relevant public activity in last year; 0: generic role only |
| Access strength | 10 | 10: warm introduction; 6: verified direct channel; 3: social connection; 0: guessed contact |

Disqualify candidates whose business appears inactive, whose only fit is a vague job title, who require invented contact data, or whose work creates an obvious conflict with a current employer/client.

### 2.4 List-building workflow

1. Start with public cohort directories, accelerator portfolio pages, university startup-center announcements, association member spotlights, company newsrooms, and the candidate's own recent posts.
2. Record candidate, organization, role, segment, source URL, trigger, date observed, and contact provenance.
3. Confirm the person is responsible for applications, operations, portfolio support, or business development.
4. Score the record. Review all scores and remove duplicates before finding a contact channel.
5. Seek a warm introduction for scores of 80+; use direct outreach only if no introduction path exists.
6. Personalize the first two lines from the recorded trigger. If the trigger cannot be verified, do not send.
7. Work in batches of ten. Review reply and interview rates before building the next batch.

## 3. Interview screener

Use a short form or a five-minute call. The screener is for qualification, not sales.

### Intro and consent

> eunwon은 우리 회사에 맞는 정부지원사업을 찾고 신청 준비까지 관리하는 서비스를 만들고 있습니다. 현재 업무 방식을 배우기 위한 30분 인터뷰이며, 인터뷰 참여가 상품 구매를 의미하지 않습니다. 답변은 제품 개선 목적으로만 사용하고, 이름이나 회사가 드러나는 인용은 별도 허락 없이 공개하지 않습니다.

Ask for permission before recording. A refusal to record must not affect participation.

### Founder questions

1. 지난 12개월 동안 정부지원사업을 몇 번 찾아보거나 신청했나요? `0 / 1 / 2–3 / 4+`
2. 다음 60일 안에 검토하거나 신청할 공고가 있나요? `예 / 아니요 / 잘 모르겠음`
3. 현재 공고는 주로 어디에서 찾나요? `기업마당 / K-Startup / 검색·커뮤니티 / 컨설턴트·지인 / 기타`
4. 가장 시간이 많이 드는 단계는 무엇인가요? `공고 탐색 / 자격 판단 / 준비서류 / 사업계획서 / 마감·진행관리 / 기타`
5. 이 업무를 실제로 담당하는 사람은 누구인가요? `대표 / 팀원 / 외부 전문가 / 공동`
6. 현재 사용하는 자료나 화면을 인터뷰 중 보여줄 수 있나요? `예 / 아니요`
7. 유용하다면 실제 공고 하나를 대상으로 유료 파일럿을 검토할 의향이 있나요? `예 / 조건부 / 아니요`

Founder priority: a real application in 60 days plus a repeated pain in eligibility, documents, or deadline management. Do not exclude zero-application candidates from research, but do not offer them the paid Sprint.

### Advisor/accelerator questions

1. 현재 몇 개 회사의 지원사업 탐색 또는 신청 준비를 돕나요? `1–9 / 10–19 / 20–49 / 50+`
2. 한 달에 검토하는 공고나 신청 건수는 어느 정도인가요?
3. 고객/포트폴리오별 자격, 문서, 마감, 진행 상태를 무엇으로 관리하나요?
4. 반복 작업이 가장 많은 단계는 무엇인가요?
5. 실제 워크플로 일부를 인터뷰 중 보여줄 수 있나요?
6. 10개 회사 대상 진단 또는 30일 파일럿을 검토할 권한이 있나요? 없다면 누가 결정하나요?
7. 고객 데이터를 외부 도구에 입력할 때 필요한 보안·계약 조건은 무엇인가요?

Advisor priority: 20+ served companies or 10+ recurring active clients, repeated workflow, and access to the decision maker.

### 4.4 Reply handling

- Positive: send two time choices, screener link, purpose, privacy note, and meeting link.
- Referral: thank the sender, research the referred person independently, and mention the introduction accurately.
- “Send information”: send a one-page overview and ask one qualifying question; do not attach a feature dump.
- Not now: ask permission to reconnect once at a specific future month.
- No/opt-out: acknowledge once, mark `do_not_contact`, and stop.

## 5. Thirty-minute interview guide

### 0–3 minutes — context and consent

- Thank the participant and restate: research first, no purchase obligation.
- Confirm role and responsibility for support-program work.
- Ask permission to take notes and, separately, to record.
- Explain that names, company identifiers, and quotations are not published without separate written permission.

### 3–10 minutes — last real instance

Ask for the most recent actual application or search, not opinions about an imagined future.

1. “마지막으로 지원사업을 찾거나 신청했던 때를 처음부터 이야기해 주세요.”
2. “무엇이 계기가 되었고, 누가 먼저 공고를 발견했나요?”
3. “어떤 사이트, 문서, 사람을 오갔나요?”
4. “각 단계에 누가 참여했고, 어디에서 멈추거나 되돌아갔나요?”

### 10–18 minutes — pain, trust, and alternatives

1. “자격이 맞는다고 판단할 때 가장 믿는 근거는 무엇인가요?”
2. “틀린 판단의 비용은 무엇이었나요?”
3. “준비서류, 사업계획서, 마감은 지금 어디에 기록하나요?”
4. “현재 방식에 돈을 내고 있나요? 외부 전문가 비용도 포함해 주세요.”
5. “그 방식에서 유지하고 싶은 점과 바꾸고 싶은 점은 무엇인가요?”
6. “AI가 만든 내용에서 반드시 사람이 확인해야 하는 부분은 어디인가요?”

For advisors, add: “기업별 권한, 감사기록, 자료 반출, 고객 동의 중 필수 조건은 무엇인가요?”

### 18–24 minutes — concept test

Show the current product; do not lead with a pitch.

1. Ask the participant to explain what they think the product does.
2. Give one realistic program and ask where they would click first.
3. Show source-cited eligibility, gap analysis, checklist, deadline/status, and document draft.
4. Ask: “무엇을 믿기 어렵나요?”, “무엇이 없어 실제 업무에 못 쓰나요?”, “현재 방식 대신 쓸 수 있는 지점은 어디인가요?”
5. Do not defend the interface. Record observed behavior separately from stated preference.

### 24–28 minutes — willingness to pay

1. “실제 마감이 있는 공고 하나를 이 방식으로 준비한다면 누가 예산을 결정하나요?”
2. “직접 하는 비용과 외부 도움 비용을 생각했을 때, 유료로 검토 가능한 범위는 어느 정도인가요?” Ask open-ended before showing price.
3. Present the Application Sprint exactly as defined below.
4. Ask for a decision: `yes`, `needs approval`, `not now`, or `no`; capture the reason verbatim internally.

### 28–30 minutes — close

- Summarize the top two problems heard and ask if the summary is accurate.
- Ask permission for one follow-up and, if appropriate, one relevant introduction.
- Never request a testimonial during the interview. Seek case-study permission only after a completed pilot.

### Interview evidence standard

Within 24 hours, record:

- exact workflow steps and tools;
- frequency and recent instance date;
- pain severity and consequence;
- current spend or time, if volunteered;
- trust/security requirements;
- purchase decision and blocker;
- observed behavior versus stated preference;
- no more than three anonymized quotes, marked as internal-only until permissioned.

## 6. Paid Application Sprint pilot

### Audience and price

- For a founder with one active, suitable program and a deadline at least 14 days away.
- Pilot price: **₩149,000 including VAT**, paid before kickoff. If tax treatment differs, the founder must approve the final invoice language before sale.
- Capacity: first 10 accepted pilots; one company and one program per Sprint.

### Deliverables

1. A structured profile and source-linked eligibility review using the current official notice.
2. A gap list that separates confirmed, not-met, and unknown requirements.
3. A program-specific preparation checklist and deadline plan.
4. One AI-generated business-plan draft in the formats currently supported by eunwon (DOCX/HWPX), clearly marked as a draft requiring applicant review.
5. One 45-minute review session to walk through evidence, unknowns, and next actions.
6. One revision pass based on factual corrections supplied within three business days.

### Timeline and boundaries

- Kickoff within two business days of payment; first package within three business days after complete inputs.
- The customer remains responsible for official eligibility confirmation, factual accuracy, submission, signatures, attachments, and deadlines.
- The Sprint does not include legal/accounting advice, agency representation, application submission, selection guarantees, unlimited rewriting, market research, financial projections, or custom design.
- Sensitive documents are not requested in the initial pilot unless a reviewed secure upload and retention process exists. Use the minimum company information necessary.
- If eunwon determines before kickoff that the selected program is clearly unsuitable, offer another program or a full refund. Post-kickoff cancellation/refund terms must be approved and published before accepting payment.

### Acceptance and learning agreement

Before payment, confirm in writing:

- the selected program and known deadline;
- deliverables, timeline, price, VAT treatment, and refund terms;
- no-guarantee and customer-review responsibilities;
- permitted data, retention, and deletion path;
- permission for product analytics;
- optional, separate permission to request feedback after delivery. Case-study/logo permission is never bundled with purchase.

### Success criteria for the first 10 pilots

- At least 3 of 10 qualified interviewees purchase at the presented price.
- At least 8 of 10 paid pilots reach package delivery.
- Median founder delivery time is tracked; target gross margin above 70% after direct AI and operator time.
- At least 70% of delivered pilots use one execution action after delivery (checklist completion, export, next action, or status change).
- Record refund, correction, and trust incidents. Do not turn these targets into external claims.

## 9. Weekly operating cadence and gates

### Week 1

- Build and review the first 10 founders and 8 advisors.
- Approve messaging, sender identity, calendar link, privacy note, invoice/refund language, and CRM access.
- Send nothing until every record has a verified trigger and provenance.

### Week 2

- Contact the approved first batch; hold up to eight interviews.
- Review replies after ten sends. If positive reply rate is below 10%, pause and inspect candidate fit and opening lines before sending more.

### Weeks 3–4

- Complete the 30-founder/20-advisor list and remaining interviews in batches.
- Offer the paid Sprint only to qualified candidates with a live application need.
- Review every pilot for expectation, correction, privacy, and delivery incidents.

### Decision gates

- Continue founder recruitment if at least 40% of completed interviews describe a repeated eligibility/preparation problem and at least 3 of 10 qualified interviewees buy the Sprint.
- Refine the offer if pain is repeated but purchase intent is weak; compare urgency, authority, scope, and price before adding features.
- Prioritize partner workflow if advisor interviews show 20+ client portfolios, repeated manual operations, and at least two organizations accept a scoped pilot.
- Stop a segment if repeated interviews show no active need or no credible buyer; do not compensate with more volume.

## 10. Setup required from the founder before outreach

Only these approvals are necessary:

1. Sender name, title, business email, and signature.
2. A scheduling link and available interview windows.
3. Approved privacy/recording language and a restricted place for interview notes.
4. CRM owner and access policy.
5. Business registration, invoicing/VAT, payment, refund, and customer-support details before accepting a paid pilot.
6. Explicit approval for the first 20 outbound messages. No external contact or publication is authorized by this document.
