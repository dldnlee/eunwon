# Batch 01 executive review

Reviewed: 2026-09-06  
Decision: **Not approved for sending**

## Why

The 50-account prospect file is a useful research queue, but it is not yet an outreach list. The first verification pass produced three candidates suitable for founder review, all advisor organizations. The other seven candidates lack either a current support-program need, a responsible recipient, or an appropriate business channel.

Sending the three advisor drafts alone would distort the learning plan, while promoting weaker founder leads would create avoidable trust and deliverability risk. No external message should be sent from this batch yet.

## Approval gate

Batch 01 must contain exactly 10 individually qualified accounts:

- 6 founder companies and 4 advisor or accelerator organizations;
- no more than 2 accounts from one source cohort;
- no more than 3 accounts from one industry or organization type;
- account-specific score of at least 65, recalculated from cited evidence;
- one live first-party source and one additional current source;
- an explicit active workflow or a trigger observed within the previous 90 days;
- a named responsible role;
- a verified business channel or warm introduction appropriate for research outreach;
- no do-not-contact signal or known conflict;
- a personalized opening supported by the cited evidence;
- one learning hypothesis and one qualifying question.

Drafts must not claim eligibility, customer outcomes, time savings, funding likelihood, or advisor capabilities that have not been built and verified.

## Operating capacity for the first learning cycle

- Maximum 8 discovery interviews per week.
- Maximum 2 paid Application Sprint kickoffs per week.
- Reserve about 60 minutes for preparation, interview, and notes per discovery call.
- Reserve 4–6 delivery hours per paid Sprint until actual delivery time is measured.

## Release rule

Approve the batch only when all 10 rows pass every gate. After approval, send in two waves of five, at least one business day apart. Review delivery failures and replies before releasing the second wave. Stop the sequence if the wrong-recipient rate exceeds 20%, any recipient reports a trust or privacy concern, or the offer cannot be delivered within the stated capacity.
