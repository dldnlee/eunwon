## Review standard

A candidate moves to `ready_for_founder_review` only when the public evidence supports all of the following:

1. the organization is currently operating;
2. the trigger is current and relevant;
3. a named responsible role or clearly scoped program/business inbox is public;
4. the channel is appropriate for a partnership or research request, rather than customer support for an unrelated product;
5. there is no guessed email, private data, or unsupported claim in the draft.

Company age, location, active application timing, workflow frequency, and buying authority remain interview qualifications unless a source states them. A prepared draft does not authorize sending.
