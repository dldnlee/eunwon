# Eunwon Weekly Operating System

Last updated: 2026-09-05

## Objective and current constraint

Build Eunwon toward **US$100,000 in recognized revenue over 12 months**, using a planning rate of
US$1 = ₩1,350. The operating target is therefore **₩135M recognized revenue**, with a **₩145M cash
collections stretch target** to absorb foreign-exchange movement, refunds, and payment failures.

These are targets, not achieved results. Until the scorecard contains observed data, the company's
single constraint is **unproven repeat usage and willingness to pay**, not missing feature breadth.

The approved revenue model is a free acquisition product followed by:

- Solo: ₩39,000/month or ₩390,000/year.
- Advisor: ₩149,000/month or ₩1.49M/year.
- Founding Application Sprint pilot: ₩149,000 once for the first 10 qualified customers, with a
  defined evidence checklist and human review. The intended standard price is ₩590,000, but it is
  not launched until pilot delivery time, gross margin, and willingness-to-pay evidence support it.

No success-fee offer is authorized. The current free access remains until the monetization gate is
met, and early activated users receive a 60–90 day grandfather period when paid access begins.

## Weekly scorecard

Every metric has a target, an observed actual, a prior-period comparison, and a named owner. Unknown
actuals remain `Not measured`; they are never entered as zero or inferred from product activity.

| Area | Weekly measures | Initial decision threshold | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Qualified visitors, signups, signup rate, source | Signup rate ≥ 8% | CMO |
| Activation | Completed profiles, first useful match, saved program, activation rate | Signup-to-activation ≥ 55% | CTO |
| Value | Applications advanced, documents started, reported hours saved | Trend and cohort baseline first | CEO |
| Retention | Week-1 and week-4 retained users, notification-driven returns | Week-4 retention ≥ 35% | CEO |
| Sales | Qualified Advisor leads, demos, proposals, wins, Sprint sales | Advisor demo-to-paid ≥ 20% | CEO |
| Revenue | Collections, recognized revenue, MRR, annual recurring revenue, revenue by offer | Against monthly target | CFO |
| Quality | Eligibility corrections, stale notices, failed notifications, support incidents | No unresolved high-severity trust incident | CTO |
| Economics | AI spend, AI cost per activated/paid user, CAC, gross margin, Sprint delivery hours | See cost controls below | CFO |
| Cash | Opening cash, cash in, cash out, closing cash, three-month commitments | Three months of forward expense before hiring | CFO |

### Cost controls

- Pre-revenue AI ceiling: ₩100,000/month; alerts at 50% and 75%, with nonessential AI work stopped
  at 100%.
- AI allocation: 40% ingestion, 50% customer actions, and 10% incident reserve.
- After monetization, AI cost remains below 5% of recognized revenue.
- Non-founder operating expense remains at or below ₩800,000/month through day 90.
- Paid acquisition is not scaled until one channel demonstrates at least 3× gross-profit LTV:CAC
  and payback below three months.

## Decision gates

1. **Turn on paid packaging:** at least 100 activated users, 30 week-4 retained users, and 10
   documented willingness-to-pay interviews.
2. **Continue paid pilots:** at least half of pilot customers report measurable time savings and at
   least 30% renew or prepay.
3. **Scale acquisition:** one channel meets the LTV:CAC and payback rules above; activation and
   retention thresholds are sustained by cohort.
4. **Scale eligibility coverage:** the controlled evidence pilot passes semantic and citation review.
5. **Ship customer notifications broadly:** authorized test-recipient delivery succeeds for each
   notification class.
6. **Add a roadmap item:** it has evidence that it improves activation, trust, retention, revenue,
   or material cost reduction.
7. **Add contractors or recurring commitments:** three months of the resulting forward operating
   expense is already held in cash.

## 30/60/90 priorities

### Days 1–30

- Establish trustworthy funnel and AI-cost telemetry from visit through application progress.
- Interview 30 prospective customers across founders, SMB operators, accountants, consultants,
  accelerators, and support organizations.
- Personally onboard 10–15 design partners and observe their first-value journey.
- Deliver five concierge application workflows and measure time saved.
- Fix only activation, trust, and reliability blockers; choose one primary segment from evidence.

### Days 31–60

- Sell and manually support 10 Solo design partners, five Advisor customers, and five paid
  Application Sprints.
- Run the controlled eligibility-evidence pilot and notification-delivery verification.
- Establish one repeatable partner/referral motion and document objections, close rate, and effort.
- Build multi-company workflow only after Advisor commitments demonstrate demand.

### Days 61–90

- Launch paid packaging to new users if the monetization gate passes; grandfather the early cohort.
- Standardize Sprint scope, turnaround time, evidence checklist, and expert-review capacity.
- Publish two consented, evidence-based case studies without eligibility or funding guarantees.
- Run one bounded acquisition experiment each week and stop channels that miss decision thresholds.

## Owner cadence

| Cadence | CEO | CTO | CMO | CFO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday operating review | Set the single constraint and three priorities; own pipeline | Report activation, quality, incidents, and delivery capacity | Report channel funnel and planned experiments | Report cash, revenue, expenses, and budget variance |
| Wednesday customer review | Lead interviews, demos, and offer decisions | Review product friction observed in sessions | Synthesize messages, objections, and channel evidence | Review pricing and margin impact of proposed offers |
| Friday execution review | Decide stop/continue/iterate and update forecast | Report shipped learning and reliability status | Report experiment outcomes and next test | Close weekly collections and update forward commitments |
| Monthly board review | Own segment, people, and company-priority decisions | Own trust, security, performance, and technical risk | Own cohort/channel performance and positioning | Own close, P&L, runway, unit economics, and forecast |

Only one company backlog is authoritative. Functional plans serve the current constraint and do not
create independent roadmaps.

## Founder setup dependencies

These are the only founder-controlled prerequisites to the current plan:

1. Confirm Korean business registration, business bank account, and Toss merchant readiness.
2. Establish bookkeeping/VAT support and obtain review of subscription and Sprint refund terms.
3. Designate a government-support application expert for paid Sprint reviews.
4. Reserve recurring time for customer interviews, Advisor demos, and approval of public case studies.
5. Approve the initial discretionary spending ceiling of ₩800,000/month and any exception before it
   is committed.
